# Assumptions and Constraints

## Assumptions
- Medicines are manually entered, with no barcode or automated inventory system
- Staff skill levels vary, and data entry accuracy can drop during busy OPD hours
- The clinic operates under constant time pressure with overlapping tasks
- Doctor time is extremely limited and should not be used for routine operational work

## Constraints
- No new software or ERP changes are allowed
- Only existing tools (HMS, Google Sheets, Google Forms) can be used
- All daily controls must run within 30 minutes
- Doctor involvement must be minimal, structured, and exception-based
